    Sides

    Space to pause, full stop to single-step, F to re-fit, Ctrl+A to cycle the arcs drawn: none, where each gun can point, and where it will fire (amber when on target). C to colour rounds and beams by the side that fired them. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a ship to pick it out (its target is marked, the arcs are only its, and F frames the two of them; either one off screen or too small is shown close up in a window by it, which a click frames), Escape to let it go. Shift-click or Shift-drag to pick more of a side, double-click for all of a design, A for the whole side; right-click an enemy to attack it or a friend to defend it, with Shift to add to their orders; T, D and R arm attack, defend and ram for the next click, S stops. Blue, red, green and magenta are the sides, grey a side beyond the fourth or a hulk. The ring marks where the gravity well pulls at 1 m/s².Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap a ship to pick it out and tap empty space to let it go. Blue, red, green and magenta are the sides.
